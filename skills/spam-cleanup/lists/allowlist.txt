# Spam-cleanup allowlist — senders/domains to ALWAYS rescue from Spam.
# One entry per line.
# An entry is a full address (name@example.com) or a bare
# domain (example.com). A domain matches that exact domain and its subdomains,
# on DNS label boundaries only: `example.com` matches `mail.example.com` and
# does NOT match `notexample.com`, `example.community`, or
# `example.com.evil.ru`. Never substring-match here — lookalike domains are
# cheap to register and substring matching is how they get treated as trusted.
# Lines starting with '#' are comments.
#
# The skill appends here only on your explicit, per-entry approval, and only for
# a sender that BOTH authenticates (dkim/dmarc pass, aligned to the From domain)
# AND has prior two-way correspondence. A single convincing inbound message is
# never enough — an allowlisted domain auto-rescues out of Spam forever, so a
# poisoned entry here is a durable compromise. Prefer a full address over a
# domain when the relationship is with a person rather than a service.
